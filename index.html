    How to play Antichess

    1. Lose to win. Get rid of all your pieces, or get stuck with no legal moves, and you win.
    2. Captures are forced. If you can capture, you must. If several captures are possible, you choose which. The pieces you can take are shown in red.
    3. The king is just a piece. There's no check or checkmate, kings can be captured, and there's no castling.
    4. Pawns can promote to a king, as well as a queen, rook, bishop or knight.

    Tip: give your pieces away by moving them where your opponent will be forced to take them.

    A game is drawn after 50 moves each with no capture or pawn move, when the same position comes up 3 times, or when only bishops are left that can never meet.
